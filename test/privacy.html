What We Collect

When you use ContentClik, we collect information you give us directly. This includes your name, email address, organization, and the topics you follow. We also collect information automatically when you browse, including your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent.

When you fill out a content access form, your details may be shared with the publisher of that content. We tell you this clearly at the point of submission.

How We Use Your Information

We use what you share to make ContentClik work for you. That means sending you the weekly briefings you signed up for, showing you relevant content, remembering your preferences, and improving the platform over time.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for purposes you have not agreed to.

Our main uses include:

  • Delivering content, briefings, and platform features you have requested
  • Sharing your form submissions with publishers whose content you accessed
  • Improving how we serve content and manage the platform
  • Sending you updates about ContentClik when you have opted in

Who We Share It With

We share your information with publishers when you submit an access form for their content. This is the core of how our platform works. Publishers receive your professional details to follow up with you about their content.

We use trusted third-party services to run the platform, including Firebase for data storage and authentication, and email services for our briefings. These providers only use your data to provide us with their services.

We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Cookies

ContentClik uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences, and understand how the platform is being used. You can control cookie settings in your browser at any time.

We use strictly necessary cookies that are required for the platform to function, and optional analytics cookies that help us understand how to improve. We ask for your consent before using optional cookies.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can also ask us to stop using your data for marketing at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@contentclik.com. We respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We take security seriously. Your data is stored on secure servers managed by Firebase and protected by industry-standard encryption. We limit who within our team can access personal data and review our security practices regularly.

No system is completely without risk. If you notice something that does not look right, let us know immediately.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy or how we handle your data? Reach out to us at privacy@contentclik.com. We are a small team and we reply personally.
